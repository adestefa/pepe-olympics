THE FIELD MANUAL

It’s all in the timing.

Use Watch demo to see the keys light up with TAP/HOLD prompts. Press any gameplay key or Take control to take over. Press Let’s vault and wait for the three beeps. Tap D for the left foot and L for the right. Alternate at a steady rhythm to build speed; holding a key does not repeat a step. Tap H for a quick pole adjustment, or hold it for about a third of a second to lower the pole fully. Releasing H keeps the position. Press Space anywhere along the runway to plant. Keep holding it to store extra spring power: up to two seconds gives the biggest launch. Releasing Space keeps both hands on the pole. The lime strip is a reference; an early plant needs more speed to reach the bar.

Momentum keeps carrying Pepe forward. When the timing cue turns green, press and hold J to boost the swing and bring his feet up. Each attempt gets one J boost, so time the first press. Then tap K on its green cue to extend and throw. K commits the release; there is no second Space press. Wait too long and the swing loses energy.

Set Body weight between attempts: 50–180 kg, starting at 95 kg. A lighter frog accelerates faster and flies higher; a heavier frog needs more steps and gets less boost from the same pole. Gravity stays the same. Faster steps, good J/K timing and a long Space hold can throw Pepe past the mat, into the wall, or out of the arena. For a controlled landing, use a short Space hold and plant earlier.

Start at 3–4.2 m. The crossbar falls when hit. R restarts; P pauses the game and every sound. Press it again to resume. Touch controls provide the same actions. Audio begins after your first interaction.
